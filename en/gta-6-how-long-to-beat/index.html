How Long Is GTA 6 – Story Length and 100% Completion Time

GTA 6 is the longest game in the series. How long will the story take you, and how long for a full 100%?

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answers
  • Official: about 80 hours – that's how long it took Rockstar's Rob Nelson, with some optional content.
  • Kotaku's reviewer: about 90 hours to the final mission, with a lot of side content.
  • Story only: pre-release leaks pointed to about 50 hours. There's no HowLongToBeat data yet.
  • 100%: estimates go as high as 200 hours (leak, unconfirmed).

Beating GTA 6 takes about 80 hours – based on the account of Rockstar's Rob Nelson, who finished the campaign with some optional content. Kotaku's reviewer reached the final mission after about 90 hours. It's the longest story game in the studio's history.

Playstyle Time Source

To the finale with lots of side content about 90 hrs Kotaku review

Compared with other Rockstar games

Game Story (approx.)

The figures for GTA 5 and RDR2 are typical times from sites that collect player data – they depend on how you play.

What makes the game longer?

  • 91 main missions across 5 chapters,
  • Jason and Lucia's relationship missions,
  • robberies of stores, banks and gang hideouts,
  • collectibles and activities (trading cards, races, meditation, hunting, the zoo),
  • the 100% completion tracker, which unlocks after the finale.

How long does the Platinum take?

The Platinum requires 100% completion, so realistically you're looking at more than 100 hours. Details in our GTA 6 trophies guide.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours is GTA 6?

According to Rockstar's Rob Nelson, playing through the campaign with some optional content takes about 80 hours. Kotaku's reviewer reached the finale after about 90 hours, doing a lot of side content.

How long is the GTA 6 main story?

There's no official figure for the story alone. Pre-release leaks pointed to about 50 hours if you focus on main missions. Player data (e.g., HowLongToBeat) will come in over the next few weeks.

Is GTA 6 longer than GTA 5?

Yes, significantly. GTA 5's story usually took about 30–35 hours, and RDR2's about 50 hours. GTA 6 is Rockstar's longest story game.
