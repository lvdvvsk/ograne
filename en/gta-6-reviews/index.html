GTA 6 Reviews – Scores, Metacritic and What Critics Say

GTA 6 has one of the highest review averages in gaming history. Here are the scores, the most common pros and cons and the key critic verdicts.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answers
  • Metacritic: 99/100 – tied with The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the highest score ever.
  • OpenCritic: 98/100 and 100% of critics recommend it.
  • 10/10 from IGN, GameSpot, Edge, Game Informer and others. 5/5 from Eurogamer.
  • Most common complaint: 30 fps and frame drops on the base PS5.

GTA 6 has a 99/100 on Metacritic and a 98/100 on OpenCritic. Its 99 ties The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the highest score in the site's history – on PS5, a whopping 118 of 147 reviews are perfect scores. The review embargo lifted on November 18, the day before launch.

What do reviewers praise?

  • Jason and Lucia's relationship – the couple is the heart of the story, with well-written dialogue.
  • Leonida's living world – the density of detail, NPCs, wildlife, weather.
  • The chase and wanted system – police react to witnesses and suspect descriptions.
  • Heists – from stores to banks, with multiple ways to pull them off.
  • Driving – heavier and more realistic than in GTA 5.
  • Multiple endings and choices with consequences.

What do they criticize?

  • 30 fps on all consoles – no 60 fps mode.
  • Frame drops on the base PS5 – down to about 20–25 fps in demanding scenes.
  • Weak enemy AI and low mission difficulty.
  • A slow start (ScreenHub).

Reviews from Polish outlets

  • PPE.pl – 10/10. The reviewer tested the game on PS5 and PS5 Pro and advises base PS5 owners to "knock off a point" until performance patches arrive.
  • Gry-Online published its review after 120 hours of play, calling GTA 6 more than just game of the year.

Frequently asked questions

What is GTA 6's Metacritic score?

GTA 6 has a 99/100 on Metacritic (PS5 version, 147 reviews, including 118 perfect scores). That ties it for the highest score in the site's history, alongside The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.

Is GTA 6 good?

According to critics – it's outstanding. Reviewers praise Jason and Lucia's relationship, the dialogue, the living world, the chases and the heists. The most common complaints are the 30 fps cap, frame drops on the base PS5, weak enemy AI and low mission difficulty.

What was the lowest GTA 6 review score?

In TechPowerUp's roundup, the lowest score was 80/100 from ScreenHub, which complained about the game's slow start.
