About Us and Contact

Ograne.com is an independent fan site about Grand Theft Auto VI. We publish guides, explainers and fact roundups about the game – this is the English edition.

Author

The site is run by Patryk Ładowski – a content creator with a background in Polish YouTube, a video editor and a social media specialist. He's been playing GTA since the San Andreas days and writes guides based on his own gameplay and Rockstar's official materials.

How we fact-check

  • We base our facts on official announcements from Rockstar and Take-Two and on reputable outlets (Polish and international). We list our sources under every article.
  • Information from a single source or from leaks is clearly marked as unconfirmed.
  • Spoilers are hidden behind buttons.
  • Every article shows its last updated date.
