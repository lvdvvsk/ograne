GTA 6 Interactive Map – Vice City and All of Leonida

Zoom, filter and search more than 900 places in GTA 6. Every pin shows the real Florida building it's based on.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 22, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answers
  • 928 places on the Leonida map in 11 categories: hotels, shops, transport, nature, public buildings and more.
  • Real-world counterparts: each place shows the Miami or Florida building it was modeled on.
  • Search and filters, fullscreen mode and a shareable link for every pin.
  • The base map is a community reconstruction (gtadb.org, CC BY 4.0) from pre-release material – the in-game layout may differ.

This interactive GTA 6 map shows 928 places across the state of Leonida – from Vice Beach hotels to the ports and gas stations of the Leonida Keys. Every pin lists its district, category and the real Florida building that inspired it. You can zoom, filter and search the whole map.

    The counterparts for Vice City, the Keys and Grassrivers are obvious. For the other regions, these are community guesses, not Rockstar's words.

    Vice City – the heart of the GTA 6 map

    Vice City is Leonida's biggest city and its "sun-soaked vacation capital." You'll find the beaches of Ocean Beach, nightlife, luxury mansions, a container port and an international airport. Confirmed locations include Little Cuba, the Tisha-Wocka flea market, Vice City Port and Vice City International Airport.

    According to pre-release previews, Vice City alone is roughly twice the size of Los Santos from GTA 5. It's the series' first return to the city since GTA: Vice City (2002) and Vice City Stories (2006).

    Leonida Keys – where the game begins

    The story kicks off in the Leonida Keys – a chain of islands linked by long bridges and coastal roads. Rockstar describes the waters there as some of the most beautiful and dangerous in America. This is where Jason works for local smugglers, and the rest of the map only opens up later.

    📍 Show on the map: Leonida Keys

    ★ Described places in this region

    • ★ Jason's House – Jason's chapter 1 safehouse, owned by Brian. Renovate it before Lucia comes home, meditate on the balcony and read 4 magazines here. Warning: you lose the house for good after Tourist Trap. Read the guide →
    • ★ The Rusty Anchor – The bar from Coming Home and Day Release: the party after Lucia gets out of prison, a live band and a brawl. Read the guide →
    • ★ Brian's Boat Works & Marina – Brian Heder's marina – the start of Down to Earth and Swamped. Brian's stash is the first fence in the game (after Lab Fees), but he pays very little. Read the guide →
    • ★ 24/7 Supermarket – The 24/7 store in Key Lento. One of the collectible magazines (RX Men) sits on the newspaper rack. Read the guide →
    • ★ Donovan Roeder Jr. Community Center – The community center where Lucia works. In Clock Out, pick her up before 4 PM – it improves the couple's relationship. Read the guide →
    • ★ Air Force Radar Station – The radar station on Maroon Key – where Hey Arnold! with Brian and Cal takes place, with a choice at the end. Read the guide →
    • ★ Gambit Bay – Gambit Bay – the easiest place to meet Cool Dog. Get stranded at sea without a boat, climb onto his jet ski and stay on: he takes you to Dog Island (about $1,200). Read the guide →

    Grassrivers – swamps and alligators

    Grassrivers is a wetland full of rivers, mangroves and wildlife. The best way to get around the canals is by airboat. It's the region where you'll run into alligators and "stranger discoveries" – as Rockstar itself put it.

    📍 Show on the map: Grassrivers

    ★ Described places in this region

    • ★ Main Prison Building – Leonida Penitentiary in Hamlet. The prologue starts here (you play as Lucia), and Jason picks her up here in Coming Home. Read the guide →
    • ★ Fleeca – The Payday Loan in Hamlet – one of the prologue locations. Later, Payday Loans are robbery targets (7 needed for 100%). Read the guide →
    • ★ Pelican River – Pelican River in the Mariana County swamps – Kirk's Skunk Ape (Bigfoot) missions and the Find the Lost Drugs task. Alligators bask here. Read the guide →
    • ★ Grassrivers Visitor Center – Grassrivers Visitor Center – the gateway to the swamps. Use an airboat or hovercraft to get around the canals. Read the guide →
    • ★ Casino – The casino in Buckskin. Nearby, along the road through Buckskin Swamp, are the Kowaachobee bar with pool, King Gator mini golf and the Swamp meditation. Read the guide →

    Port Gellhorn – the forgotten coast

    Rockstar calls Port Gellhorn "Leonida's forgotten coast." It's a former resort town left with cheap motels, shuttered attractions and empty malls. On the west coast there are docks, warehouses and a rail hub. The local economy runs on drugs, booze and energy drinks.

    📍 Show on the map: Port Gellhorn

    ★ Described places in this region

    • ★ Starlet Motel Sign – Starlet Motel – the chapter 2 safehouse. East of here, the UFO appears at 2:00 AM (trophy "The Truth is Out There"). Read the guide →
    • ★ Thrillbilly Mud Club – Thrillbilly Mud Club – Billy's storyline starts here (Thrillbillies). 10 wins here, at Gellhorn Raceway and Boone Speedway unlock the vehicle repair cheat. Read the guide →
    • ★ Gellhorn International Raceway – Gellhorn International Raceway – a race track modeled on Sebring. One of three tracks that count toward the vehicle repair cheat. Read the guide →
    • ★ Pawn & Gun – Pawn & Gun in Port Gellhorn – buy weapons and ammo here. Read the guide →
    • ★ Delights – Delights club by the interstate. North of it, near Moses' workshop, is the free Port Gellhorn Station garage (after Daily Driver). Read the guide →
    • ★ Manifest Canyon – Manifest Canyon – red rocks with the Red Rocks meditation, near where the train tracks cross the highway. Read the guide →

    Ambrosia – a one-factory town

    Ambrosia lives off a single company: the Allied Crystal sugar refinery. The Final Chapter biker gang takes care of the rest of the local "services." The town sits on Lake Leonida, surrounded by farmland and factories.

    📍 Show on the map: Ambrosia

    ★ Described places in this region

    • ★ Sugar Mill – The Allied Crystal sugar mill – Ambrosia's only big employer. The rest of the town is run by the Final Chapter biker gang. Read the guide →
    • ★ Ambrosia Raceway Park – Ambrosia Raceway – demolition derby. A win pays about $3,800–6,300, and you can rent a car for a few hundred dollars. Read the guide →
    • ★ Leonard County Sheriff's Office – Belville in Leonard County. The house from the GTA San Andreas photo stands in this area – its garage hides a conspiracy theorist's den with notes about "J" and "L". Read the guide →
    • ★ Evergreen Mall – Evergreen Mall in Waning Sands. Nearby are Frontier Adventure Golf, the Mount Hailes Club course and the Seaside meditation on the beach to the east. Read the guide →

    Mount Kalaga – the national park

    In the north of the state lies Mount Kalaga National Park: mountains, forests and river valleys. Rockstar promises hunting, fishing and off-road trails here. It's the wildest part of the map, with a vibe reminiscent of Red Dead Redemption 2.

    📍 Show on the map: Mount Kalaga

    ★ Described places in this region

    • ★ Mount Kalaga – The summit of Mount Kalaga – a stone circle with the final, 18th meditation (Ascent Mountain). It unlocks after all the others. Read the guide →
    • ★ Lake Tallaloko – Lake Tallaloko. Northwest of here, above a waterfall, is the Waterfall meditation. Read the guide →
    • ★ Jacob's Tavern Bar – Jacob's Tavern in Millport – a bar with pool next to a Pay 'n' Spray. There's also a book about a New Austin cowboy here, a nod to Red Dead Redemption. Read the guide →

    How to use the map

    1. Zoom and pan – mouse wheel, the +/− buttons or pinch on mobile.
    2. Search – type a place, district or real building (e.g. “Ritz”, “airport”, “Key”).
    3. Filter – untick categories you don't need. “All” and “None” toggle every filter at once.
    4. Click a pin – see its details, real-world counterpart and a link you can share.
    5. Fullscreen – the button above the map fills your screen.

    Nearby pins are grouped into numbered circles. Click a circle or zoom in to expand it.

    Where the data comes from

    Rockstar hasn't published a full in-game map. The base map and locations come from gtadb.org (GTA VI Landmarks Map) and the YANIS GTA VI Community Map, pieced together by fans from trailers, screenshots and Street View comparisons. The data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 – attribution is shown in the corner of the map.

    It's a reconstruction from pre-release material, so roads, coastlines and some building positions may differ from the final game. We'll update the map as new data arrives. You can download the official Leonida map poster from Rockstar Games.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this an official GTA 6 map?

    No. Rockstar hasn't released a full in-game map – only an official poster of Leonida. This map is a reconstruction by gtadb.org and the GTA VI Mapping Community, built from trailers and pre-release material and licensed under CC BY 4.0.

    How many places are on the map?

    928 named places in 11 categories. Each one lists its district, category and – where known – the real Florida building it's based on.

    How do I find a specific place on the GTA 6 map?

    Type its name into the search box above the map – it also works with real-world names like “Ritz” or “airport”. Click a result and the map zooms to the pin.

    Does the map work on mobile?

    Yes. Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, and use the “Fullscreen” button to fill the screen.

    Where can I download the official GTA 6 map?

    The official Leonida map poster is in the Downloads section of Rockstar Games' site: rockstargames.com/VI/media.
