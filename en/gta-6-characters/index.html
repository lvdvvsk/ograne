GTA 6 Characters – Lucia, Jason and the Full Cast

The series' first lead duo, a Keys smuggling crew and Vice City's music empire. Here's who's who in GTA 6.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answers
  • Protagonists: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos – a Bonnie and Clyde-style criminal duo.
  • Character switching: you can swap between Jason and Lucia at any time, including during missions.
  • Voice actors (per the credits): Jason – Dylan Rourke, Lucia – Manni L. Perez.
  • Supporting cast: Cal Hampton, Brian and Lori Heder, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, Real Dimez, Raul Bautista.

The protagonists of GTA 6 are Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos – a couple who, after a botched "easy score," get caught up in a conspiracy that reaches Leonida's highest circles. You can switch between them at any time. Around them is a colorful cast: smugglers from the Keys, Vice City music bosses and a daredevil bank robber.

Lucia Caminos

Lucia is the first character we see in the game – she starts the story in Leonida Penitentiary. She ended up there after defending her family. Her father taught her how to fight. With some luck she got out early, but she's wearing an electronic ankle monitor, and the first voice we hear is her probation officer's.

Lucia dreams of the stable life her mother once talked about back in Liberty City. Roselyn Sanchez is credited as her mother, Mia Caminos.

  • Voice: Manni L. Perez (per the end credits).
  • Playstyle: hand-to-hand combat, robberies, quick decisions.

Jason Duval

Jason grew up surrounded by grifters and small-time crooks. After serving in the Army he returned to the Leonida Keys and makes extra money working for local smugglers – running drugs and collecting debts. He wants an easy life, but it keeps slipping further away. He lives rent-free at Brian Heder's place in exchange for "favors."

  • Voice: Dylan Rourke (per the end credits).
  • Playstyle: shooting, driving, jobs for the Keys crew.

How does character switching work?

  • You can switch at any moment – in the open world and during missions.
  • Your partner can tag along on activities.
  • Shared fail state: if one of you dies, you both fail.
  • Splitting up during a chase lowers your wanted level by one star.
  • You keep your money in a shared bank account, accessed at ATMs.

Other key characters

  • Captain Mendez – a Vice City cop who knows the couple and plays a big role in the story.
  • Wyman – a mechanic at a scrapyard garage who gives out classic car jobs.
  • Phil – owner of Phil's Ammu-Nation (he resembles a younger Phil Cassidy from earlier games).
  • DWNPLY – a rapper Real Dimez recorded a hit with (voiced by Denzel Curry).
  • Andrés de León – a wealthy man tied to the Megamundo telenovela storyline.

Celebrities in the GTA 6 cast

Rockstar brought some big names into the game. According to the end credits, they include:

  • Bad Bunny as Mitico Mario,
  • Denzel Curry as DWNPLY,
  • Freddie Gibbs as Freddie,
  • Tyreek Hill (NFL star) as Maleek Rivers,
  • Sexyy Red as herself,
  • Michael Jai White as Kordell Grant.

The main cast includes more than 60 names. Some supporting actors' names looked garbled in early lists, so we're leaving a few characters marked "unconfirmed" until we've checked the in-game credits.

Frequently asked questions

Who is the main character in GTA 6?

GTA 6 has two playable protagonists: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Lucia is the first woman to lead the story in the series' modern 3D era.

Who voices Lucia and Jason in GTA 6?

According to the end credits, Jason is played by Dylan Rourke and Lucia by Manni L. Perez. As usual, Rockstar didn't announce the cast before launch.

Can you switch characters in GTA 6?

Yes. You can switch between Jason and Lucia at any moment, both in the open world and during missions. If one of them dies, you both fail.

Are there celebrities in GTA 6?

Yes. According to the credits, the game features Bad Bunny, Denzel Curry (as the rapper DWNPLY), Freddie Gibbs, Tyreek Hill and Sexyy Red, among others, while Michael Jai White plays Kordell Grant.
