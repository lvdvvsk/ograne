GTA 6 Interactive Map – Vice City and All of Leonida

Zoom, filter and search more than 900 places in GTA 6. Every pin shows the real Florida building it's based on.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answers
  • 928 places on the Leonida map in 11 categories: hotels, shops, transport, nature, public buildings and more.
  • Real-world counterparts: each place shows the Miami or Florida building it was modeled on.
  • Search and filters, fullscreen mode and a shareable link for every pin.
  • The base map is a community reconstruction (gtadb.org, CC BY 4.0) from pre-release material – the in-game layout may differ.

This interactive GTA 6 map shows 928 places across the state of Leonida – from Vice Beach hotels to the ports and gas stations of the Leonida Keys. Every pin lists its district, category and the real Florida building that inspired it. You can zoom, filter and search the whole map.

    Category filters

    Base map and locations are a community reconstruction from pre-release material – the in-game layout may differ.

    How to use the map

    1. Zoom and pan – mouse wheel, the +/− buttons or pinch on mobile.
    2. Search – type a place, district or real building (e.g. “Ritz”, “airport”, “Key”).
    3. Filter – untick categories you don't need. “All” and “None” toggle every filter at once.
    4. Click a pin – see its details, real-world counterpart and a link you can share.
    5. Fullscreen – the button above the map fills your screen.

    Nearby pins are grouped into numbered circles. Click a circle or zoom in to expand it.

    Key areas on the map

    The most mapped areas are Vice City and Vice Beach (Miami and Miami Beach), Key Lento in the Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn, Capri, Hamlet and Ambrosia. For descriptions of Leonida's six regions – Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga – see our GTA 6 map regions guide.

    Where the data comes from

    Rockstar hasn't published a full in-game map. The base map and locations come from gtadb.org (GTA VI Landmarks Map) and the YANIS GTA VI Community Map, pieced together by fans from trailers, screenshots and Street View comparisons. The data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 – attribution is shown in the corner of the map.

    It's a reconstruction from pre-release material, so roads, coastlines and some building positions may differ from the final game. We'll update the map as new data arrives. You can download the official Leonida map poster from Rockstar Games.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this an official GTA 6 map?

    No. Rockstar hasn't released a full in-game map – only an official poster of Leonida. This map is a reconstruction by gtadb.org and the GTA VI Mapping Community, built from trailers and pre-release material and licensed under CC BY 4.0.

    How many places are on the map?

    928 named places in 11 categories. Each one lists its district, category and – where known – the real Florida building it's based on.

    How do I find a specific place on the GTA 6 map?

    Type its name into the search box above the map – it also works with real-world names like “Ritz” or “airport”. Click a result and the map zooms to the pin.

    Does the map work on mobile?

    Yes. Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, and use the “Fullscreen” button to fill the screen.

    Where can I download the official GTA 6 map?

    The official Leonida map poster is in the Downloads section of Rockstar Games' site: rockstargames.com/VI/media.
