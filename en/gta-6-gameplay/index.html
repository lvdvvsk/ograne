GTA 6 Gameplay – All the Key Mechanics

A witness-based wanted system, robbable stores everywhere, a weapon limit, car theft that depends on the model year and more than 170 animal species. Here's how GTA 6 plays.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answers
  • Police only respond when someone sees or reports a crime, and they build a description of you.
  • Your criminal profile replaces RDR2's honor system and shapes how the world reacts to you.
  • Every store can be robbed; bigger targets include jewelers, banks and gang hideouts.
  • Weapons: you carry a max of 2 pistols and 2 long guns.
  • Money: you sell loot to fences and deposit cash at ATMs.

GTA 6 goes all in on realism: police react to witnesses and build a description of you, you can rob any store, you carry a limited number of weapons and you have to cash in your loot with fences. On top of that there's a phone woven into the world, more than 170 animal species and hurricanes. This rundown of the mechanics is based on press previews and launch coverage.

Wanted system: witnesses and suspect descriptions

  • Stars only appear when someone sees the crime, the victim reports it or a camera records it.
  • Police create a suspect profile: face, outfit, weapon, vehicle. The HUD shows what they know about you.
  • 6 wanted levels. Hollow stars – police are looking for an unknown suspect. Solid stars – they know it's you.
  • Cops usually don't show up on the minimap, and the search covers a "threat zone."
  • How to lose the cops: change your clothes or hairstyle, ditch your weapon or car, put on a mask, split up with your partner (−1 star) or drive through a roadblock in an inconspicuous car.

Criminal profile

The criminal profile replaces Red Dead Redemption 2's honor system. It tracks whether you operate professionally or violently. It affects how hostile NPCs are, how harshly police respond and – according to guides – the ending. Extreme violence can permanently "crack" the profile icon.

Heists and robberies

  • Every store in Leonida can be robbed.
  • Bigger targets: jewelers, small banks and gang hideouts.
  • Cash from a bank may be marked – you'll have to sell it to a fence.
  • You can blow open a safe (faster, but a smaller take) or slowly crack it.
  • How much you can carry depends on your bag and vehicle.

Weapons and combat

  • You carry a max of 2 concealed pistols and 2 long guns. The rest waits in an Ammu-Nation locker or in your car.
  • A shot to a limb incapacitates an enemy.
  • The reticle color tells you the outcome: white – hit, yellow – incapacitated, red – kill.
  • Focus, the slow-motion ability, is back.

Vehicles and car theft

  • Hundreds of vehicles: supercars, boats, kayaks, e-scooters, off-roaders, airboats.
  • Driving is heavier than in GTA 5, cars use fuel and you charge EVs at charging stations.
  • Stealing depends on the model year: you can pick the lock or smash the window on older cars, while newer ones require a key cloner or hacking.
  • A stolen car is "temporary" until you register it. Pay 'n' Spray and nitrous are back.

Phone and social media

  • The phone is shown in your character's hands, without a screen overlay.
  • Apps: social media, messages, banking, fitness, video, health.
  • Ryde Me is your taxi and fast travel, and Scooter Bros delivers your vehicle to you.
  • In-game posts and livestreams reveal random events – you can head over to them.

Money, fences and property

  • You can't spend jewelry, contraband and marked cash directly – you sell them to fences.
  • Cash on hand is lost when you die or get arrested, so deposit it into your shared account at an ATM.
  • You can't buy safehouses – they unlock through the story. You can buy garages.

Wildlife and weather

  • More than 170 animal species, including over 60 bird species, sharks, reptiles and invasive species.
  • The rarity system and legendary animals from RDR2 are back, and there's also a zoo.
  • Hurricanes and tropical storms, local weather and wind that moves the grass and water.

Other features

  • Photo mode from day one.
  • Manual saves – you can't replay missions from a menu.
  • Cheats only after the story – see GTA 6 cheats.

Frequently asked questions

How does the wanted system work in GTA 6?

Police only respond once a crime is seen by a witness, reported by the victim or caught on camera. Officers build a description – face, clothing, weapon and vehicle. There are 6 wanted levels. Hollow stars mean the police don't know who the suspect is; solid stars mean they've identified you.

Can you buy houses in GTA 6?

You can't buy safehouses – you unlock them through the story. You can, however, buy garages to store more cars.

Are there animals in GTA 6?

Yes, more than 170 species: over 60 bird species, over 30 land mammals, sharks, reptiles, fish and invasive species. You'll run into alligators in Grassrivers, and you can pet and feed dogs.

Does GTA 6 have a first-person mode?

According to previews, there's no full first-person mode. The first-person view only appears when aiming with certain weapons.
