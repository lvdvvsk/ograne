GTA 6 Ending – How Many Endings and What Happens After the Credits

The final mission, the point of no return, the different endings and whether you can keep playing. Spoilers are hidden – you reveal them yourself.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 6 min read

⚠️ This article contains spoilers. Key parts are hidden – click “Show spoiler” to reveal them.
Quick answers
  • Final mission: "The End of the Road" (Chapter 5), preceded by "Protective Custody."
  • Point of no return: the game asks whether you want to start the finale. Make a separate manual save at that point.
  • How many endings: more than one – major outlets saw 2–3, early guides describe 4. The exact conditions haven't been officially confirmed.
  • After the credits: the game takes you back to just before the finale, so you can keep playing and replay the ending. No New Game Plus.

GTA 6 ends with the mission "The End of the Road," in which Jason and Lucia storm Giorgio Gallo's penthouse for the hard drive – the only bargaining chip that can win them their freedom. The game has more than one ending, and after the credits it takes you back to just before the finale, so you can keep playing and try a different outcome.

How to reach the GTA 6 ending

The finale consists of the 7 missions of Chapter 5:

  1. Bird in the Hand
  2. Taking Liberties
  3. VC Baby
  4. Doggie Bag
  5. Operation Savage Glory
  6. Protective Custody
  7. The End of the Road

Before the last stretch, the game clearly warns you about the point of no return and asks whether you want to start the finale. If you have unfinished side content, you can do it now or after the credits – the world stays open.

Most important tip: missions can't be replayed from a menu, so right before the finale make a separate manual save in a new slot. That way you can easily go back and see a different ending.

What happens in the finale?

Show spoiler – how the finale plays out

After Giorgio's betrayal, Jason and Lucia end up in separate jail cells. Prosecutor Danica Howard offers a deal: freedom in exchange for the drive Giorgio is using to blackmail Senator Camila Fuentes. In "Protective Custody" the couple gets back in the game, and in "The End of the Road" they storm Giorgio's penthouse in Vice City. Along the way they have to fight corrupt cops and face off against Captain Mendez.

How many endings does GTA 6 have?

For now, one thing is certain: there's more than one ending. The number depends on the source:

Source How many variations they saw
Kotaku (review) 2, "wildly different"
VICE (as reported by Polish outlets) 3 across 4 playthroughs
Gameblog.fr (guide) 4
Rockstar "more than two," no number given (per PPE.pl)

Rockstar hasn't published an official list, so treat the descriptions below as early, unverified reports.

What determines the ending?

Guides published in the first days after launch point to these factors:

  • your choice in a key scene of the finale – the couple is surrounded and has to decide what to do next,
  • Jason and Lucia's relationship – how much you developed it in optional scenes,
  • your criminal profile – whether you operated professionally or violently and recklessly,
  • savings in your shared account – how much you put away,
  • decisions in certain missions, including "Hey Arnold!" in Chapter 1.

Ending variations (unconfirmed)

Show spoiler – descriptions of the ending variations

According to France's Gameblog and Brazil's GameOverDrive (their descriptions partly overlap):

  • Jason's sacrifice – in the key scene, Jason saves Lucia and takes the fall, going to prison. Lucia starts a new life with her son.
  • Best ending – as above, but with high savings Lucia is able to get Jason out and the family is reunited.
  • "No surrender" – the couple chooses to fight to the end, which ends tragically for both of them, in true Bonnie and Clyde fashion.
  • "Psychopath" variation – with a very violent criminal profile, the attempt to surrender fails.

Important: no major outlet (IGN, GameSpot, Eurogamer) has confirmed these descriptions yet, and the sources differ on details, such as the name of Lucia's son. We'll update this section once we've played through every variation ourselves.

What happens after the credits? Can you keep playing?

Yes. After the ending:

  • the game takes you back to just before the finale – the protagonists "back out" of the final job,
  • you can roam Leonida freely and finish side content,
  • the finale marker stays on the map, so you can replay the ending and see a different variation,
  • the world is "frozen" – no new major story threads appear,
  • the 100% completion tracker unlocks, along with the challenges that unlock cheats.

There's no New Game Plus, and no traditional post-credits scene either.

Trophies for the ending

Completing the final mission earns you a Gold trophy. According to the available lists, no trophy requires a specific ending, and the Platinum requires 100% completion. Full breakdown in our GTA 6 trophies guide.

Frequently asked questions

How does GTA 6 end?

GTA 6 ends with the mission "The End of the Road," in which Jason and Lucia storm Giorgio Gallo's penthouse to recover the hard drive – their only ticket to freedom. What happens to the protagonists afterward depends on which ending you get.

How many endings does GTA 6 have?

More than one. Kotaku saw two "wildly different" endings, VICE's reviewer reportedly saw three variations across four attempts, and French outlet Gameblog describes four. Rockstar hasn't given an official number.

What determines the ending in GTA 6?

According to early guides, the factors are: your choice in a key scene of the finale, Jason and Lucia's relationship, your criminal profile (professional or violent), savings in your shared account and certain mission decisions. No major outlet has confirmed the full list of conditions yet.

Can you keep playing after beating GTA 6?

Yes. After the credits, the game returns you to the moment just before the finale and you can roam the open world freely. The final mission marker stays on the map, so you can replay the ending.

Does GTA 6 have New Game Plus?

No. GTA 6 has no New Game Plus mode and no traditional post-credits scene. Finishing the story does unlock the 100% completion tracker and the challenges that unlock cheats.

Do any trophies depend on the ending?

According to the available trophy lists, no trophy requires a specific ending – you only need to complete the finale once.
