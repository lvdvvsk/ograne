GTA 6 Performance – FPS, Resolution and PS5 vs PS5 Pro

Does GTA 6 run at 60 fps? How does it look on the base PS5 versus the PS5 Pro? Here are the facts from testing.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answers
  • 30 fps on every console. There's no 40 or 60 fps mode.
  • PS5: about 1440p, with drops to about 20–25 fps in demanding scenes.
  • PS5 Pro: PSSR close to 4K, better ray tracing, a more stable 30 fps, more cars and pedestrians.
  • Xbox Series X|S: no detailed testing available at the time of publication.

GTA 6 runs at 30 frames per second on all consoles – there's no 60 or 40 fps mode. On the base PS5 the game renders at around 1440p and can drop to about 20–25 fps in demanding scenes. The PS5 Pro upscales the image close to 4K, adds ray tracing and holds a more stable 30 fps.

Console versions compared

PS5 PS5 Pro Xbox Series X / S
Target frame rate 30 30 30
60 fps mode no no no
Resolution about 1440p about 1440p + PSSR close to 4K not yet tested
Ray tracing basic enhanced (lighting, reflections) not yet tested
Stability drops to about 20–25 fps more stable 30 fps not yet tested

Why only 30 fps?

Even before launch, technical analysts predicted that the bottleneck would be the consoles' CPU – simulating crowds, traffic, wildlife and weather in such a dense world puts a heavy load on the processor. Rockstar prioritized detail over frame rate.

What does the PS5 Pro add?

  • PSSR upscaling to a near-4K image,
  • stronger ray tracing (global illumination and reflections),
  • more cars and pedestrians on the streets,
  • longer draw distance and more intense weather,
  • a more stable 30 fps.

Tips for base PS5 owners

  • Turn on your TV's game mode to reduce input lag.
  • Adjust motion blur in the settings – at 30 fps it has a big impact on how the game feels.
  • Keep an eye out for patches – reviewers are hoping for performance fixes in the first few weeks.

The game also needs up to 212 GB of storage. More on platforms in our GTA 6 release date article.

Frequently asked questions

Does GTA 6 have a 60fps mode?

No. GTA 6 runs at 30 frames per second on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S. There's no 40 or 60 fps performance mode.

Is it worth playing GTA 6 on PS5 Pro?

Yes, if you have the choice. The PS5 Pro holds a more stable 30 fps, upscales the image close to 4K with PSSR and has stronger ray tracing. The base PS5 has some frame drops.

What resolution does GTA 6 run at on PS5?

According to launch analyses, around 1440p internally, targeting 30 fps. During explosions and heavy traffic, frame rates drop to about 24–25 fps.
