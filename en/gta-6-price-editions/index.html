How Much Is GTA 6? Prices and Editions Compared

Two editions, a physical version with no disc and a pre-order bonus. Here's what GTA 6 costs and whether the Ultimate Edition is worth the extra money.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answers
  • GTA 6 Standard: $79.99 on PS Store and Microsoft Store.
  • GTA 6 Ultimate: $99.99 – extra vehicles, weapons, outfits, missions and businesses.
  • Physical edition: the box only contains a download code, no disc. It may cost more at retailers than the digital version.
  • No collector's edition has been announced.

GTA 6 costs $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition (PlayStation Store and Xbox Store prices). Local store prices vary by region. The physical edition has no disc – the box only contains a download code.

GTA 6 price list

Edition Price (digital, USD) What you get
Standard $79.99 full game
Ultimate $99.99 full game + bonus content bundle
Physical (Standard) varies by retailer download code in a box

The physical edition is just a code in a box and may cost more at retailers. Store prices can vary and drop during sales such as Black Friday.

Pre-orders opened on June 25, 2026, and $79.99 is the new standard for AAA games – Rockstar previously sold its games for $59.99.

What's in the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition?

Rockstar described Ultimate as an "exclusive collection" of items for Story Mode. According to the announcements, the bundle includes:

  • unique vehicles – Polish sources list, among others, the Grotti Cheetah '95, the Dinka Enduro motorcycle, the Shitzu Squalo boat and the Vapid Dominator Buggy '67 with a garage,
  • a pair of revolvers for Jason and Lucia, plus customized pistols,
  • outfits, tattoos and extra customization for weapons, cars and clothing,
  • additional missions and businesses (including the Rideout Customs garage) – this part comes from Polish store descriptions, so double-check the product page before you buy.

Ultimate doesn't change the story or the endings. It's cosmetics and a head start.

Is Ultimate worth the extra $20?

Short answer: only if you like collecting cars and outfits. The extra vehicles and the business give you a faster financial start, but the game is complete without them. If you're playing once for the story, Standard is more than enough. The price of upgrading from Standard to Ultimate hadn't been announced at launch.

GTA 6 physical edition – no disc

Rockstar went with a "code-in-a-box" release. When you buy the boxed version in a store, you get a download code, not a Blu-ray disc. What that means:

  • you still download up to 212 GB from the internet,
  • consoles without a disc drive (PS5 Digital, Xbox Series S) aren't a problem,
  • reselling a used copy is practically impossible once the code has been redeemed.

Pre-order bonus: Vintage Vice City Pack

Everyone who ordered the game before November 20, 2026 got a pack that nods to the original GTA: Vice City:

  • a 1955 Vapid Stanier sedan,
  • a garage in Ocean Beach,
  • retro outfits and hairstyles,
  • a weapon livery based on Tommy Vercetti's Hawaiian shirt.

Some Polish outlets also reported a month of GTA+ with digital orders – that comes from a single source.

Where to buy GTA 6 for the lowest price

  1. PlayStation Store / Microsoft Store – base price $79.99, instant download.
  2. Electronics retailers (boxed code) – may cost more at first, but sales can make it cheaper than digital.
  3. Watch out for "GTA 6 PC keys" – there is no PC version, so any such offer is a scam. More in our GTA 6 on PC article.

Frequently asked questions

How much does GTA 6 cost?

GTA 6 costs $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition on PlayStation Store and Xbox Store. Local store prices vary by region.

What's the difference between GTA 6 Ultimate and Standard?

The story and the world are the same. Ultimate adds an item bundle: unique vehicles, a pair of revolvers for Jason and Lucia, extra customization for weapons, cars and clothing and – according to Polish store listings – additional missions and businesses.

Is GTA 6 on a disc?

No. The physical edition only contains a code to download the game. You'll need an internet connection and up to 212 GB of free space to install it.

Is GTA 6 on PS Plus or Game Pass?

There's no official word that GTA 6 has been added to PS Plus Extra, Premium or Game Pass. It's a new full-price game.

What was the GTA 6 pre-order bonus?

The Vintage Vice City Pack: a classic 1955 Vapid Stanier sedan, a garage in Ocean Beach, retro outfits and hairstyles and a weapon livery inspired by Tommy Vercetti's shirt. It applied to orders placed before November 20, 2026.
