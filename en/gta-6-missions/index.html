GTA 6 Mission List – How Many Missions and How the Chapters Break Down

How many missions GTA 6 has, how they're split across chapters and which ones to know about before the finale. No story spoilers in the names of hidden missions.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answers
  • Main missions: 91 across 5 chapters (22 / 11 / 26 / 25 / 7), according to Red Bull Gaming's list.
  • Other sites list 90 or 94 – the difference comes down to whether the prologue and side missions are counted.
  • Finale: "Protective Custody" and "The End of the Road."
  • Missions can't be replayed from a menu – use manual saves.

GTA 6 has 91 main missions split across 5 chapters – according to Red Bull Gaming's list. The longest is Chapter 3 (26 missions), and the shortest is the final Chapter 5 (7 missions). The last mission is "The End of the Road."

Mission tips

  • Make a manual save before every big decision. GTA 6 has no mission replay menu.
  • Keep an eye on your criminal profile. Violent approaches change how police and NPCs react – and, according to guides, the ending too.
  • Put money in your shared account. Cash you carry is lost when you die or get arrested.
  • Do Jason and Lucia's relationship missions. They're optional, but they may matter in the finale.

Frequently asked questions

How many missions are in GTA 6?

According to Red Bull Gaming's mission list, GTA 6 has 91 main missions split across 5 chapters. Some sites list 90 (without the prologue) or 94 (with additional missions).

What is the last mission in GTA 6?

The final story mission in GTA 6 is "The End of the Road" in Chapter 5. It's preceded by the mission "Protective Custody."

Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

Not from a menu. The only way is to load an earlier manual save, so it's worth saving in several slots. The exception is the finale – its marker stays on the map after the credits.
