GTA 6 Cheat Codes – How to Unlock and Enter Cheats on PS5 and Xbox

Cheats are back in GTA 6, but not right away. You have to unlock them first, and using them comes at a cost. Here's how it works.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answers
  • Cheats only become available after you finish the story – each one has to be unlocked separately through a challenge.
  • Number of cheats: 61 (player, combat, vehicles, environment, visual filters).
  • How to enter them: on your character's phone, with a sequence of D-pad directions.
  • The cost: using a cheat disables trophies and saving until you quit the game.

GTA 6 does have cheats, but they only work after you finish the story. There are 61 of them, each one has to be unlocked with its own challenge, and you enter them with the D-pad on your character's phone. Using a cheat disables trophies and saving until you quit the game.

How to unlock cheats in GTA 6

  1. Finish the main story (final mission: "The End of the Road").
  2. Open the progress menu and go to the cheats tab.
  3. Each cheat has its own challenge – for example, you unlock the raise-wanted-level cheat by losing the cops multiple times.
  4. Once you've met the requirement, the combination appears in the menu.

That's a big change from GTA 5, where cheats worked from the very start.

How to enter a cheat

  • Open the phone (D-pad up).
  • Switch to cheat entry mode – according to GTABase you hold L1 and press R1, while HotHardware says to hold all triggers and bumpers. Follow the in-game prompt.
  • Enter the sequence with the D-pad. The combinations are identical on PS5 and Xbox.

Cheats confirmed by only one source may turn out to be inaccurate. We'll test them and add more categories to the list: vehicles, weapons, weather and visual filters.

Cheat categories

  • Player (10) – money, health, wanted level.
  • Combat (11) – ammo, weapons, Focus.
  • Vehicles (21) – spawning cars, motorcycles, boats.
  • Environment (3) – weather and time.
  • Visual filters (16) – visual effects.

What you lose by using cheats

  • Trophies and achievements are disabled until you quit the game.
  • Saving is turned off for that session.
  • Your existing save stays untouched – just quit the game to go back to normal play.

Frequently asked questions

Are there cheats in GTA 6?

Yes, GTA 6 has 61 cheats. However, they only unlock after you complete the main story, and each cheat has its own unlock challenge.

How do you enter cheats in GTA 6?

Open the phone with D-pad up, go to the cheats screen and enter the sequence using the D-pad. The combinations are the same on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Sources differ on which buttons you need to hold – follow the on-screen prompt in the game.

What is the GTA 6 money cheat?

The cheat for $250,000 is: up, right, down, left, up, left, down, right. It unlocks after you finish the story.

Do cheats disable trophies in GTA 6?

Yes. After you use a cheat, trophies and saving are disabled until you quit the game. Cheats don't affect your existing save, though.
