GTA 6 Release Date – When, What Time and on Which Platforms

GTA 6 is out now. Here's everything in one place: the release date, unlock time, platforms, languages and what we know about the PC version.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answers
  • GTA 6 release date: Thursday, November 19, 2026.
  • Unlock time: midnight – the game unlocked at 12:00 a.m. local time in each region (rolling launch).
  • Platforms: PlayStation 5 (with PS5 Pro enhancements) and Xbox Series X|S. It's not on PC, and no date has been announced.
  • Languages: English is one of 13 supported languages (subtitles and menus); dialogue is in English.
  • Online: Story Mode only at launch; GTA Online for GTA 6 has no date yet.

Delay history: from 2025 to November 2026

The road to launch was a long one. Rockstar officially revealed the game with its first trailer in December 2023, targeting 2025 at the time.

  1. Fall 2025 – the original release window.
  2. May 26, 2026 – the new date announced in May 2025, alongside the second trailer.
  3. November 19, 2026 – the final date, which Take-Two announced on November 6, 2025 with its quarterly earnings. Rockstar said the extra months were needed to polish the game.

Almost three years passed between the first trailer and launch, and more than 13 years since GTA V (2013). That's the longest gap between numbered entries in the series.

What time did GTA 6 come out?

Rockstar used a rolling launch, meaning the game went live in waves across time zones. It unlocked at 12:00 a.m. local time, so:

  • players in New Zealand and Australia were first,
  • each region got access at midnight local time on November 19, 2026,
  • in the US, launch fell on the night from Wednesday to Thursday, local time.

Pre-loading opened on November 10, 9 days before launch. You needed to free up up to 212 GB of storage – the exact size depends on the console.

What platforms is GTA 6 on?

GTA 6 is exclusive to current-gen consoles:

  • PlayStation 5 – the base version, 30 fps.
  • PlayStation 5 Pro – the same game with enhancements: PSSR upscaling close to 4K, stronger ray tracing and a more stable 30 fps. More in our article on GTA 6 performance.
  • Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S.

The game isn't available on PS4 or Xbox One. According to Sensor Tower estimates, about 75% of copies sold on PlayStation and about 25% on Xbox.

Is GTA 6 on PC?

No. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version and hasn't given any date. The studio's track record suggests PC gets its games later: GTA V came to PC a year and a half after consoles, and Red Dead Redemption 2 a year later. Beware of "GTA 6 PC installers" – they're scams and malware. Details in our GTA 6 on PC article.

What languages does GTA 6 support?

English is one of 13 supported languages for subtitles and menus. The spoken dialogue is in English – just like in GTA V and RDR2. The supported languages include English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.

Does GTA 6 have an online mode?

At launch, GTA 6 is a single-player-only game. Rockstar hasn't announced a date for the online mode. Bloomberg reports that GTA Online for GTA 6 may arrive in 2027, but that's unofficial. The existing GTA Online (for GTA V) is still running. More: GTA 6 Online – what we know.

Frequently asked questions

When did GTA 6 come out?

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) launched on Thursday, November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

What time did GTA 6 unlock?

At midnight local time on November 19, 2026. Rockstar unlocked the game at 12:00 a.m. local time in each time zone, so players in Asia and Australia got to play several hours before Europe and the Americas.

Is GTA 6 on PS4 or Xbox One?

No. GTA 6 only runs on current-gen consoles: PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. It isn't available on PS4 or Xbox One.

What languages is GTA 6 available in?

GTA 6 supports 13 languages for subtitles and menus, including English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese. The spoken dialogue is in English.

How many times was GTA 6 delayed?

Twice. The game was supposed to come out in fall 2025, then on May 26, 2026, and it finally hit shelves on November 19, 2026.
