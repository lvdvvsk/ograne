GTA 6 on PC – Is It Out and When to Expect It

Short answer: GTA 6 isn't on PC, and there's no date. Here's what Take-Two says, what Rockstar's history tells us and how to avoid getting scammed.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answers
  • GTA 6 isn't on PC. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version or given a date.
  • There are no system requirements – any "GTA 6 requirements" table is guesswork.
  • History: GTA 5 came to PC about 19 months after consoles, RDR2 about 13 months after.
  • Beware of scams: GTA 6 PC "installers" and "keys" are scams and malware.

GTA 6 on PC doesn't exist – Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version or given a date. The game came out on November 19, 2026 only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. There are no official system requirements either.

What does Take-Two say?

In May 2026, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said that Rockstar has announced GTA 6 "only for consoles for now." He added that nearly all of the company's hits eventually come to every platform, and that the PC market is growing for "console-type" games. That's a hint, not an announcement.

When will GTA 6 come to PC? Lessons from history

Game Consoles PC Gap
GTA 5 September 2013 April 2015 about 19 months
Red Dead Redemption 2 October 2018 November 2019 about 13 months
GTA 6 November 2026 ? ?

If Rockstar follows the same pattern, PC will get the game late 2027 at the earliest, and realistically it could be 2028. That's just extrapolation, though.

GTA 6 PC system requirements

There are no official requirements. The tables you see online are estimates based on console specs. A hint: on PS5 the game runs at about 1440p and 30 fps and takes up to 212 GB – so a PC port will likely need a fast SSD and a powerful CPU.

Beware of "GTA 6 PC" scams

After launch, fake "PC versions" started popping up online – including paid ones. The rule is simple:

  • any GTA 6 PC "installer," "repack" or "key" is a scam,
  • these files often contain password-stealing malware,
  • official PC news will come from the Rockstar Newswire and from Steam, Epic and the Rockstar Games Launcher.

How to play GTA 6 if you only have a PC

  • PS Remote Play – stream from your own PS5 to your computer.
  • Xbox Remote Play – the same for Xbox consoles.
  • Cloud gaming – if Microsoft or Sony officially makes the game available in the cloud.

Until then, there's GTA 5 and GTA Online on PC – still supported by Rockstar.

Frequently asked questions

Is GTA 6 on PC?

No. GTA 6 came out on November 19, 2026 only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version.

When is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Nobody knows. Take-Two says the game has been announced only for consoles "for now," and that the company's hits eventually come to all platforms. Based on GTA 5 and RDR2, a port could arrive in 2027 or 2028 – but that's speculation.

What are the GTA 6 PC system requirements?

There are no official requirements, because a PC version hasn't been announced. Sites publishing "GTA 6 requirements" are giving their own estimates.

Can you play GTA 6 on a computer?

Not natively. The only options are streaming from your own console (e.g., PS Remote Play) or cloud gaming, if it's ever officially offered.
