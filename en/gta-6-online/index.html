GTA 6 Online – When Multiplayer Is Coming and What Happens to GTA Online

GTA 6 launched as a single-player-only game. Here's what we know about multiplayer and what's happening with the current GTA Online.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answers
  • GTA 6 at launch = single-player only.
  • GTA 6 Online release date: no official date. Bloomberg (Jason Schreier) points to 2027 – unofficially.
  • The current GTA Online (for GTA 5) is still running and will continue to be supported.
Contents
  1. What do we know about GTA 6 Online?
  2. Why did Rockstar release the story mode on its own?

GTA 6 Online didn't launch alongside the game and has no official release date. On November 19, 2026, Rockstar released GTA 6 as a single-player-only game. According to Bloomberg, the online mode is expected in 2027, but the studio hasn't confirmed that.

What do we know about GTA 6 Online?

  • At launch: nothing. Rockstar describes GTA 6 as a single-player experience.
  • Release date: not announced.
  • Reports: in October 2026, Jason Schreier (Bloomberg) reported that multiplayer will arrive in 2027. The head of Twitch had hinted at something similar earlier.
  • Business model: officially unknown – it's not clear whether it'll be a separate game or a free mode.

Why did Rockstar release the story mode on its own?

The studio hasn't explained it directly. The most common explanations in the media are a desire to polish the campaign after two delays, and the fact that the current GTA Online still makes huge amounts of money. Those are interpretations, though, not official statements.

What about the current GTA Online?

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said that GTA Online for GTA 5 will continue to be supported after GTA 6's launch. The servers are up, and updates are expected to keep coming.

We'll revisit this topic as soon as Rockstar shares details about the online mode.

Frequently asked questions

Does GTA 6 have an online mode?

Not at launch. GTA 6 came out on November 19, 2026 as a single-player-only game. Rockstar hasn't announced a date for the online mode.

When is GTA 6 Online coming out?

There's no official date. Bloomberg's Jason Schreier reported that multiplayer is expected in 2027, but Rockstar hasn't confirmed it.

Will GTA Online for GTA 5 shut down?

No. Take-Two's CEO said the current GTA Online will continue to be supported after GTA 6's launch.
