GTA 6 Sales – Launch Records by the Numbers

GTA 6 broke GTA 5's records before it even launched. How much did it make out of the gate, and where do these numbers come from?

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answers
  • Sensor Tower estimate: about $1.84 billion and 19 million copies by launch day (including pre-orders).
  • GTA 5 in 2013: $815.7 million and 11.2 million copies in 24 hours.
  • Platforms: about 75% PlayStation, 25% Xbox.
  • Take-Two hasn't released official figures yet – these are estimates from analytics firms.

According to Sensor Tower estimates, GTA 6 made about $1.84 billion and sold about 19 million copies by launch day, including pre-orders. That's more than double GTA 5's record-breaking launch. Take-Two hasn't published official figures yet, so all the numbers below are estimates.

GTA 6 vs GTA 5 – launch by the numbers

GTA 5 (2013) GTA 6 (2026, estimate)
Launch revenue $815.7 million (24 hrs) about $1.84 billion (by launch day)
Copies 11.2 million (24 hrs) about 19 million
$1 billion after 3 days about a week before launch
Price $59.99 $79.99

The comparison isn't perfect: GTA 6 costs more, and its numbers cover a long pre-order period.

Platform split

About 75% of sales were on PlayStation and 25% on Xbox. That reflects the larger PS5 install base and GTA 6's co-marketing with Sony.

Forecasts

Analysts at Wedbush Securities forecast $3 billion and 30 million copies by the end of 2026.

Stock market reaction

Take-Two shares rose more than 6% on the day the review embargo lifted (November 18), to about $216.61. Media estimates put the game's development budget at more than $1 billion.

We'll update these figures once Take-Two releases official numbers.

Frequently asked questions

How much money has GTA 6 made?

According to Sensor Tower estimates, GTA 6 made about $1.84 billion and sold about 19 million copies by launch day, including pre-orders. Take-Two hasn't confirmed these numbers yet.

Did GTA 6 break GTA 5's record?

According to estimates, yes – by a wide margin. GTA 5 made $815.7 million in its first 24 hours in 2013. GTA 6 passed $1 billion in pre-orders alone, about a week before launch.

How much did GTA 6 cost to make?

No official budget has been disclosed. Media estimates put the development cost at more than $1 billion.
