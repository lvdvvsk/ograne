GTA 6 Trophies and Achievements – How to Get the Platinum

How many trophies GTA 6 has, whether any are missable and what it takes to get the Platinum. A stress-free plan for 100%.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answers
  • 55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze. On Xbox, 55 achievements worth 1000 G.
  • The Platinum requires 100% completion (a Gold trophy for 100%).
  • No online or DLC trophies.
  • Cheats disable trophies until you quit the game.

GTA 6 has 55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. On Xbox, that's 55 achievements worth 1000 G. The Platinum requires 100% completion, and the list has no online or DLC trophies.

GTA 6 trophies by the numbers

Type Count
Platinum 1
Gold 2
Silver 6
Bronze 46
Total 55

You get one Gold trophy for completing the final story mission and the other for 100% completion.

Example trophies

  • Master of None – try each of the 9 activity types,
  • Not Kudos – complete 20 different races,
  • Don't Touch My Deck – collect 3 sets of trading cards,
  • Positive Mental Attitude – meditate with Lucia in Chapter 1 (flagged as potentially missable – unconfirmed).

Platinum roadmap

  1. Play through the story normally, without cheats. Make a separate save before the finale.
  2. Complete the finale once – no trophy requires a specific ending.
  3. After the credits, the 100% completion tracker unlocks – use it to knock out the rest.
  4. Save cheats for the very end, because they disable trophies until you quit the game.

How long will it take? Realistically more than 100 hours – see how long to beat GTA 6.

Frequently asked questions

How many trophies does GTA 6 have?

GTA 6 has 55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. On Xbox, that's 55 achievements worth a total of 1000 G.

Is the GTA 6 Platinum hard?

It's not technically difficult, but it's long – it requires 100% completion, which realistically means more than 100 hours. There are no online or difficulty trophies.

Are there missable trophies in GTA 6?

Most trophies can still be earned after the credits. The trophy for meditating with Lucia in Chapter 1 has been flagged as potentially missable, but that's unconfirmed.
