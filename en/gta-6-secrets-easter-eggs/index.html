GTA 6 Secrets and Easter Eggs That Are Actually in the Game

Not a trailer recap – these are secrets players found after launch, with the place, the time and what you get. Plus cameos fans of the series will love.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 22, 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answers
  • UFO: Kelly County east of Port Gellhorn, near the Starlet Motel, at 2:00 AM, from chapter 2 – The Truth is Out There trophy.
  • Bigfoot (Skunk Ape): a full side-mission chain with Kirk in the swamps, with a choice at the end.
  • Cool Dog: get stranded at sea in Gambit Bay – a dog on a jet ski takes you to Dog Island and digs up about $1,200.
  • Belville house: a photo hidden in GTA San Andreas DE since 2021 – the garage holds a conspiracy theorist's hideout.

The biggest GTA 6 secrets found since launch are the UFO (with a trophy), Bigfoot in Kirk's missions, Cool Dog on his jet ski, Dexter's apartment and the Belville house teased in San Andreas back in 2021. Below is each one with its location and conditions. We only list what several independent sources confirm; single-source claims are clearly marked.

UFO – location and trophy

What Details
Location Kelly County, east of Port Gellhorn, near the Starlet Motel
Time 2:00 AM game time
Requirement chapter 2 unlocked
Reward bronze trophy "The Truth is Out There"

The UFO abducts a pedestrian – help them to unlock the trophy. The easiest way is to wait for night at the motel.

Bigfoot – the Skunk Ape ("Mister Mangrove")

This isn't a single easter egg but a whole side-mission chain with Kirk, a cryptid hunter from Leonida's swamps: spores, branch effigies, a mutated boar, a nest and a helicopter chase. Key facts:

  • it starts with a flyer, for example at Skeeter's bar in Hamlet,
  • "Search for the Skunk Ape" and "Help Kirk Find Mr. Mangrove" work only at night,
  • the final mission is Lucia-only,
  • at the end the game makes you choose: shoot the creature or shoot Kirk.

Cool Dog – the jet ski dog

One of the funniest secrets in the game. Get stranded at sea without a boat and you'll soon hear barking – a dog in goggles and a life jacket arrives on a jet ski. Players call him Cool Dog or Lucky Dog.

  1. Take a boat far from shore – Gambit Bay (Leonida Keys) works most reliably.
  2. Jump into the water and swim away so there's no other vehicle nearby.
  3. Wait for the dog and climb onto the back of the jet ski.
  4. Don't get off until the jet ski stops completely, or you'll miss Dog Island.
  5. On Dog Island, throw the dog a ball – he digs up about $1,200 for you (players report $1,218).

You can ride the same jet ski back to the mainland. It's one of the quickest cash boosts early in the game.

Dexter Morgan's apartment

Vice City is Rockstar's Miami, so the studio recreated the building from "Dexter". It stands on Washington Island in eastern Vice City, between the Pay 'n' Spray and Top Fun Drones icons, and it's called Trinity Apartments – a nod to the season 4 Trinity Killer. A boat resembling the "Slice of Life" is moored at the dock. You can go there from the start of the game.

The Belville house – teased in San Andreas

In 2021, a photo of a house hung in the Lil' Probe'Inn bar in GTA San Andreas Definitive Edition. After GTA 6 launched, players found the same house in Belville (Leonard County). Its garage hides the den of a man convinced he lives inside a video game. The notes on the walls mention "J" and "L" (Jason and Lucia?) and a recurring six.

The egg on the tower

Climbing one of the tall radio towers takes about 7 minutes. At the top is a nest with painted eggs – throw one at a passing car to trigger a short cutscene. It's a player discovery reported by IBTimes, not an official in-game "secret".

Cameos and returning characters (spoilers)

Show cameos (story spoilers)
  • Phil Cassidy – the veteran from Vice City and GTA 3, tied to Ammu-Nation (seen in trailer 2).
  • Jimmy De Santa (same actor as in GTA 5, Danny Tamberelli) – waits in line outside a club; you can let him in or turn him away.
  • Valentina – appears in the story.
  • Niko Bellic? – Michael Hollick, Niko's voice from GTA IV, is credited as "The Hitman". Beebom says it's Niko in missions near the end of chapter 5; other outlets haven't confirmed it.

Still to verify (single source)

  • The "Fast & Furious" car – a green car styled after Brian O'Connor's in a garage on Vespucci Island (BGR).
  • An observation tower puzzle in the north involving QR codes, mentioned by reviewers – details aren't documented yet.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the UFO in GTA 6?

In Kelly County, east of Port Gellhorn, near the Starlet Motel safehouse. You need chapter 2 unlocked and must be there at 2:00 AM game time. The UFO abducts a pedestrian; helping them earns the bronze trophy The Truth is Out There.

Is Bigfoot in GTA 6?

Yes – as the Skunk Ape ("Mister Mangrove"). It's a side-mission chain with cryptid hunter Kirk in Leonida's swamps. Some missions are night-only and the last one is Lucia-only. At the end you choose whether to shoot the creature or Kirk.

Where is the jet ski dog in GTA 6?

Cool Dog (Lucky Dog) appears when you're stranded at sea without a boat – most reliably in Gambit Bay in the Leonida Keys. Climb onto his jet ski and stay on until it stops: he takes you to Dog Island and digs up about $1,200.

Where is Dexter's apartment in GTA 6?

On Washington Island in eastern Vice City, between the Pay 'n' Spray and Top Fun Drones icons. The building is called Trinity Apartments, and a boat resembling Dexter's is moored by the dock.
