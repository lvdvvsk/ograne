GTA 6 vs GTA 5 – The Biggest Differences

What's changed after 13 years? Comparing the map, protagonists, wanted system, weapons, playtime and price.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answers
  • Map: Leonida is about 2x bigger than Los Santos and Blaine County.
  • Protagonists: 2 (Jason and Lucia) instead of 3.
  • Police: 6 stars instead of 5, plus a witness and suspect-description system.
  • Weapons: a limit of 2 handguns + 2 long guns instead of a weapon wheel with your entire arsenal.
  • Playtime: about 80 hrs vs about 30–35 hrs.

GTA 6 has a map roughly twice as big as GTA 5's, two protagonists instead of three, 6 wanted stars instead of 5, and it's more than twice as long. After 13 years, Rockstar went all in on realism: police that react to witnesses, a weapon carry limit and heavier driving physics.

Map: San Andreas vs Leonida

GTA 5 had one big city and sprawling countryside. Leonida is a huge Vice City plus several distinct regions: islands, swamps, a run-down coast, an industrial town and a national park. According to previews, there are many times more built-up areas than in GTA 5. More: GTA 6 map.

Protagonists: trio vs duo

In GTA 5, Michael, Franklin and Trevor had separate lives. In GTA 6, Jason and Lucia are a couple – they play together, share a bank account, and if one dies, you both fail. Switching works like before, at any time.

Police: no more ducking around the corner

In GTA 5, stars appeared the moment you committed a crime. In GTA 6, police have to find out about it – from a witness, the victim or a camera. They build a description (face, clothing, weapon, car), and you can shake it by changing your outfit or vehicle. Hollow stars mean the police don't know yet who they're looking for.

Frequently asked questions

Is the GTA 6 map bigger than GTA 5?

Yes. Rockstar confirmed that Leonida is roughly twice the size of the GTA 5 map, and according to previews, Vice City alone is about twice as big as Los Santos.

How many wanted stars are in GTA 6?

Six – one more than GTA 5. Stars can be hollow (police know about the crime but don't know the suspect) or solid (suspect identified).

Does GTA 6 have first-person mode like GTA 5?

According to pre-release previews, GTA 6 doesn't have a full first-person mode – the game is played in third person, and the first-person view appears when aiming with certain weapons.
