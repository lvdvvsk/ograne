GTA 6 Story – What Jason and Lucia's Story Is About

First, spoiler-free: what GTA 6 is about. Below that, a summary of all 5 chapters – each one hidden behind a button.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 7 min read

⚠️ This article contains spoilers. Key parts are hidden – click “Show spoiler” to reveal them.
Quick answers
  • What GTA 6 is about: after a botched "easy score," Jason and Lucia get caught up in a conspiracy that reaches all the way to Leonida's state senate.
  • Structure: 5 chapters and 91 main missions (according to Red Bull Gaming's list).
  • Main plot thread: a mysterious bag containing a hard drive, with corrupt cops and a powerful smuggler on their trail.
  • Playtime: about 80 hours according to Rockstar (including optional content).

GTA 6 follows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos – a couple from the Leonida Keys who, after a botched "easy score," get caught up in a conspiracy involving corrupt cops, a cocaine smuggler and a state senator. The story spans 5 chapters and takes you from the islands in the south of the state to the penthouses of Vice City. Below you'll find a spoiler-free overview first, then chapter summaries hidden behind buttons.

GTA 6 story without spoilers

Lucia is just getting out of prison with an ankle monitor. Jason, a former soldier, earns extra cash working for smugglers in the Keys. Together they're looking for a shot at "one last score" that will let them start a normal life. The job looks simple: pick up a bag. It ends in disaster – and from that moment on, the couple is hunted both by the law and by the people who want the bag's contents back.

The main themes are loyalty in a relationship, money as a ticket to freedom and a satire of Florida: social media, influencers, corruption and the absurdities of the American Dream.

Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise

Show spoiler – Chapter 1

The game opens in prison, where we meet Lucia. After her release she joins Jason, who works for Brian Heder's smuggling crew. Someone helps Lucia get rid of her ankle monitor and offers them a package pickup. The crew loses one of its own in an ambush, and the bag pickup at the Grassrivers Visitor Center goes badly: corrupt cops stop the couple and Jason is seriously wounded. Soon someone sets their house on fire, and they escape from the hospital.

Chapter 2: Paradise Lost

Show spoiler – Chapter 2

The couple lies low in Port Gellhorn. Inside the bag they find an old statuette and a hard drive. Captain Mendez publicly blames them for the attack, turning them into the most wanted people in the area. To survive, they pull robberies – including one at the Delights Cabaret club – and eventually flee to Vice City.

Chapter 3: Tits, Ass, Drug Money

Show spoiler – Chapter 3

In Vice City, Boobie Ike gives them an apartment in exchange for work. Raul Bautista sets up a series of "tests" before bigger jobs. Side stories and new connections emerge, and Mendez organizes a raid. The chapter ends with a massive Sinfrontera National Bank heist.

Chapter 4: Vice City, Baby

Show spoiler – Chapter 4

The couple gets help from lawyer Abe Gonzalez – in exchange for favors. An operation against the VCIA ends in failure and losses for the crew. Valentina gives Raul up to Mendez, and Jason and Lucia have to face the consequences. The trail leads to Giorgio Gallo – the bag's real owner.

Chapter 5: Top of the World, Ma

Show spoiler – Chapter 5

It turns out Giorgio is using the drive to blackmail Senator Camila Fuentes into turning a blind eye to his cocaine smuggling. He promises the couple a pardon in exchange for their cooperation. After a successful but bloody job, Giorgio betrays them – Jason and Lucia end up in separate jail cells. Prosecutor Danica Howard makes it clear: they'll only get their freedom if they recover the drive. The finale is an assault on Giorgio's penthouse and a showdown with Mendez. What happens next is covered in our GTA 6 ending article.

Who dies in GTA 6?

Show spoiler – who dies during the story

Regardless of which ending you get, characters who die over the course of the story include: Donnie (Ch. 1), Dr. Bill (Ch. 2), Karim (Ch. 4), Raul (Ch. 4) and Vinny and Liz (Ch. 5). Jason and Lucia's fate depends on the ending and hasn't yet been confirmed by major outlets.

Frequently asked questions

What is the story of GTA 6?

GTA 6 tells the story of Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos – a couple of small-time criminals from the Leonida Keys. After a mysterious bag pickup goes wrong, they get caught up in a conspiracy involving corrupt police, a cocaine smuggler and a Leonida state senator.

How many chapters are in GTA 6?

The GTA 6 story is split into 5 chapters. According to Red Bull Gaming's list, they contain 22, 11, 26, 25 and 7 main missions – 91 in total.

Who is the main villain in GTA 6?

Spoiler warning: for most of the game the couple is hunted by corrupt police captain Mendez, and in the endgame Giorgio Gallo takes center stage – a smuggler and shipping magnate who is blackmailing Senator Camila Fuentes.

Is the GTA 6 story inspired by Bonnie and Clyde?

Yes. Since the first trailer, Rockstar has presented Jason and Lucia as a modern-day Bonnie and Clyde, and one of the endings (according to early guides) references this directly.
