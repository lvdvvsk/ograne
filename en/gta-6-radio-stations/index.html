GTA 6 Radio and Music – Stations and Soundtrack

From Tom Petty in the first trailer to Justice in the launch trailer. Here's what we know about GTA 6's radio stations and music.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answers
  • Original score: French duo Justice.
  • Launch trailer song: Justice – "Flower in a Hurricane" (with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard).
  • Radio stations: more than 20 – the list from fan sites is still awaiting full in-game confirmation.

The original music for GTA 6 was created by Justice, and the game has more than 20 radio stations – from pop on Flash FM to metal on V-Rock. The trailer songs are confirmed, but Rockstar hasn't officially published the full station playlists yet.

Trailer music

  • Trailer 1 (December 2023): Tom Petty – "Love Is a Long Road."
  • Trailer 2 (May 2025): songs including The Pointer Sisters – "Hot Together," Wang Chung – "Everybody Have Fun Tonight" and Tammy Wynette – "Talkin' to Myself Again."
  • Launch trailer (October 15, 2026): Justice – "Flower in a Hurricane" featuring Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard.

Trailer records

  • Trailer 1: 93 million YouTube views in 24 hours – a record that beat MrBeast.
  • Trailer 2: according to Rockstar, 475 million views across all platforms in 24 hours.

Album and soundtrack

Rockstar has announced an official album, "GTA 6: The Album" (34 tracks), and a separate release of Justice's original score.

Frequently asked questions

What radio stations are in GTA 6?

Fan sites list more than 20 stations, including Flash FM, V-Rock, Vice City FM, VCRR, Cocoteo FM, Back Country Radio and Worldwide FM. Full playlists haven't been officially published.

What song is in the GTA 6 trailer?

The first trailer (2023) uses Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road." The second (2025) features songs including The Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together." The launch trailer (October 2026) uses Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane."

Who composed the music for GTA 6?

The original score was created by the French electronic duo Justice.
