GTA 6 Map – Leonida, Vice City and Every Region

Leonida is a fictional Florida: a big city, tropical islands, gator-filled swamps and rugged mountains. Here's your guide to the GTA 6 map.

By: Patryk Ładowski · Updated: November 21, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answers
  • Setting: the fictional state of Leonida, based on Florida.
  • Main city: Vice City (the game's Miami).
  • Regions: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Mount Kalaga.
  • Size: according to Rockstar, the map is roughly twice as big as GTA 5's. No official area in square miles or km² has been given.
  • The game starts in the Leonida Keys; the rest of the map opens up as the story progresses.

The GTA 6 map is the fictional state of Leonida – a satirical version of Florida. The main city is Vice City, surrounded by the tropical Leonida Keys, the Grassrivers swamps, the run-down Port Gellhorn coast, industrial Ambrosia and the Mount Kalaga mountains. According to Rockstar, the map is about twice as big as GTA 5's.

Leonida's regions at a glance

Region Real-world counterpart What it's like
Vice City Miami metropolis: beaches, clubs, port, airport, skyscrapers
Leonida Keys Florida Keys islands linked by bridges, where the game begins
Grassrivers Everglades swamps, mangroves, alligators, airboats
Port Gellhorn Gulf Coast (fan speculation) faded resort town, motels, docks, railroad
Ambrosia Lake Okeechobee area (fan speculation) home of the Allied Crystal sugar plant and a biker gang
Mount Kalaga North Florida forests (fan speculation) national park: mountains, hunting, off-roading

The counterparts for Vice City, the Keys and Grassrivers are obvious. For the other regions, these are community guesses, not Rockstar's words.

Vice City – the heart of the GTA 6 map

Vice City is Leonida's biggest city and its "sun-soaked vacation capital." You'll find the beaches of Ocean Beach, nightlife, luxury mansions, a container port and an international airport. Confirmed locations include Little Cuba, the Tisha-Wocka flea market, Vice City Port and Vice City International Airport.

According to pre-release previews, Vice City alone is roughly twice the size of Los Santos from GTA 5. It's the series' first return to the city since GTA: Vice City (2002) and Vice City Stories (2006).

Leonida Keys – where the game begins

The story kicks off in the Leonida Keys – a chain of islands linked by long bridges and coastal roads. Rockstar describes the waters there as some of the most beautiful and dangerous in America. This is where Jason works for local smugglers, and the rest of the map only opens up later.

Grassrivers – swamps and alligators

Grassrivers is a wetland full of rivers, mangroves and wildlife. The best way to get around the canals is by airboat. It's the region where you'll run into alligators and "stranger discoveries" – as Rockstar itself put it.

Port Gellhorn – the forgotten coast

Rockstar calls Port Gellhorn "Leonida's forgotten coast." It's a former resort town left with cheap motels, shuttered attractions and empty malls. On the west coast there are docks, warehouses and a rail hub. The local economy runs on drugs, booze and energy drinks.

Ambrosia – a one-factory town

Ambrosia lives off a single company: the Allied Crystal sugar refinery. The Final Chapter biker gang takes care of the rest of the local "services." The town sits on Lake Leonida, surrounded by farmland and factories.

Mount Kalaga – the national park

In the north of the state lies Mount Kalaga National Park: mountains, forests and river valleys. Rockstar promises hunting, fishing and off-road trails here. It's the wildest part of the map, with a vibe reminiscent of Red Dead Redemption 2.

How big is the GTA 6 map?

Rockstar has made two official comparisons (in Edge magazine):

  • Leonida is roughly twice as big as the GTA 5 map,
  • its total landmass is almost as large as the GTA 4, GTA 5 and RDR2 maps combined.

Press previews added that the playable area is about 3 times bigger than RDR2's and that there are many times more built-up areas than in GTA 5. Those numbers come from journalists' reports, though, not an official spec sheet. Rockstar hasn't given the area in square miles or km², so any specific figure you see online is a fan estimate.

Counties and the neighboring state

Leonida is split into counties: Kelly County, Leonard County and Vice-Dale County. The neighboring state of Gloriana also shows up on license plates and signs – so far there's nothing to suggest you can drive there.

Weather and wildlife on the map

Leonida isn't just asphalt. The game has more than 170 animal species (over 60 bird species, sharks, reptiles and even invasive species like monkeys and armadillos) as well as hurricanes and tropical storms. Wind sways the swamp grass and ripples the water. More on the mechanics in our article on GTA 6 gameplay.

Frequently asked questions

Where does GTA 6 take place?

In the fictional state of Leonida, which is based on Florida. The main city is Vice City – the game's take on Miami, familiar to fans from 2002's GTA: Vice City.

How big is the GTA 6 map?

Rockstar confirmed to Edge magazine that Leonida is roughly twice the size of the GTA 5 map, and that its total landmass is almost as big as the maps of GTA 4, GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 combined. The studio hasn't given an exact area.

How many cities are in GTA 6?

One big city – Vice City – plus several smaller towns, including Port Gellhorn and Ambrosia. On top of that there's countryside, the Leonida Keys islands, the Grassrivers swamps and Mount Kalaga National Park.

Is the whole GTA 6 map available from the start?

No. The game starts in the Leonida Keys, and other regions unlock as you progress through the story.

Can you go to another state in GTA 6?

The neighboring state of Gloriana appears on license plates and road signs, but there's no confirmation that you can actually travel there.
