GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) is the latest game from Rockstar Games, released on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It's set in the fictional state of Leonida with Vice City at its center, and the playable protagonists are Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. The game holds a 99/100 on Metacritic and, by estimates, sold around 19 million copies by launch day.

What is GTA 6 about?

Lucia gets out of prison, while Jason makes ends meet working for smugglers in the Leonida Keys. The couple plans "one last easy score," but picking up a mysterious bag ends in disaster, and from then on they're hunted by corrupt cops and by the people who want the bag's contents back. The story spans 5 chapters and takes you from the islands in the south to the penthouses of Vice City. For the full summary (with spoilers hidden behind buttons), see GTA 6 story.

What's new compared to GTA 5?

  • a map about twice as big,
  • two protagonists instead of three, with a shared bank account and a shared fail state,
  • police with 6 wanted stars, witnesses and suspect descriptions,
  • a weapon carry limit,
  • more than 170 animal species and hurricanes,
  • a campaign of about 80 hours.

Full comparison: GTA 6 vs GTA 5.

What do critics think of GTA 6?

GTA 6 has a 99/100 on Metacritic and a 98/100 on OpenCritic. Reviewers praise the protagonists, the living world and the heists, and mostly criticize the 30 fps cap and frame drops on the base PS5. Details: GTA 6 reviews.

Frequently asked questions

When did GTA 6 come out?

GTA 6 came out on November 19, 2026. It unlocked at midnight local time in each region.

What platforms is GTA 6 on?

PlayStation 5 (with PS5 Pro enhancements) and Xbox Series X and Series S. There is no PC, PS4 or Xbox One version.

How much does GTA 6 cost?

$79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition on PlayStation Store and Xbox Store. Local store prices vary by region. The physical edition only contains a download code.

What languages does GTA 6 support?

GTA 6 supports 13 languages for subtitles and menus, including English. The spoken dialogue is in English.

Where does GTA 6 take place?

In the fictional state of Leonida, based on Florida. Its main city is Vice City (the game's version of Miami).

How big is the GTA 6 download?

Up to 212 GB, depending on your console.

Does GTA 6 have an online mode?

Not at launch. Rockstar hasn't announced a release date for GTA 6 Online. According to Bloomberg, multiplayer may arrive in 2027.
